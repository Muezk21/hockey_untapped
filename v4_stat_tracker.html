Hotkeys and rules (click to expand)
Zone entry: I Carry-in — record only when Anaheim carries the puck across the offensive blue line. The route that created the opportunity (transition, stretch pass, regroup, etc.) is not the zone entry itself.
Separate manual events: 3 DZ → OZ Transition Completed · 4 Stretch Pass → OZ Possession
Events: 1 DZ Blueline Play Shut Down · 2 D-Man Aggressive Gap Close · = OZ Turnover Forced · A Activated D-Man (OZ) · L Lost Possession
Mark the last event you logged: S scoring chance · P penalty drawn · G led to goal · Backspace undo
With a shot selected: C scoring chance · 8 2nd chance · 9 3rd+ chance · 0 traffic · - low-value · ↑ ↓ previous / next shot · Esc deselect
Shot timing is measured from the most recent Carry-in (I): Rush under 5s · Early 5–10s · Sustained over 10–30s · Extended over 30s.
Carry-in timing ends when Anaheim loses possession, the opponent shoots, a goal/stoppage/faceoff/period end occurs, or you log L, = or 1. A takeaway or giveaway that gives Anaheim possession starts a new possession, but does not count as a carry-in.
A shot with no usable entry is Unclassified. Shots after the first one in the same possession are Follow-up shots and are not timed.
Click a shot row and read the grey text under "3. Shots" to see exactly why it was classified that way.
