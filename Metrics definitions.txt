HOCKEY UNTAPPED — METRIC DEFINITIONS

DEFENSIVE PERFORMANCE

Entry Denial
A defensive action in which the defending team prevents an opponent from entering the offensive zone with controlled possession.

D-Man Gap Close
A defenseman closes the distance to an attacking opponent to reduce time, space, or available options. Record only when the defender actively closes the gap, not merely because the players are near each other.

Forced OZ Turnover
An offensive-zone defensive action in which pressure or a defensive play causes the opponent to lose possession and the defending team gains or contests the puck. Do not count an unforced opponent error as a forced turnover unless the defensive action materially contributed.

TRANSITION AND PUCK MOVEMENT

DZ → OZ Transition Completed
A sequence in which the team moves the puck from its defensive zone into the offensive zone with controlled team possession. This is a transition event, not automatically a carry-in; it does not start the entry-to-shot clock unless a separate controlled carry-in is recorded.

Stretch Pass → OZ Possession
A long pass from the defensive or neutral-zone area that reaches a teammate and results in controlled offensive-zone possession. The pass alone does not qualify if the receiving team does not establish possession.

Controlled Carry-in
An Anaheim player carries the puck across the opponent's offensive blue line while maintaining control. Exclude dump-ins, chips, uncontrolled touches, and entries where the team does not retain control. This is the entry event that starts the entry-to-shot timing clock.

OFFENSIVE INVOLVEMENT AND POSSESSION

Activated Defenseman (OZ)
A defenseman joins or advances into the offensive-zone attack beyond their normal supporting position to become an active passing, shooting, or puck-support option.

Lost Possession
The analyzed team loses control of the puck during a tracked sequence. Do not count a temporary bobble if the same team immediately retains clear control without an opponent gaining possession.

SHOT TIMING

Rush Shot
The first shot after a recorded controlled carry-in, taken 0 to less than 5 seconds after the entry.

Early Possession Shot
The first shot after a recorded controlled carry-in, taken from 5 through 10 seconds after the entry, inclusive.

Sustained Possession Shot
The first shot after a recorded controlled carry-in, taken more than 10 and up to 30 seconds after the entry, inclusive.

Extended Possession Shot
The first shot after a recorded controlled carry-in, taken more than 30 seconds after the entry.

Follow-up Shot
A subsequent shot in the same tracked sequence after the first shot. It is not assigned a new entry-timing category. Second/Third+ Chance tags are separate shot-level labels.

SHOT QUALITY AND PRESSURE

Scoring Chance
A shot manually tagged when it meets Hockey Untapped's scoring-chance criteria. Apply the same written criteria across games and reviewers; a chance tag on a non-shot manual event is not a shot-level scoring chance.

Second Chance
A follow-up shot created after the initial attempt in the same attacking sequence, such as a rebound or loose-puck recovery, and manually tagged as the second scoring opportunity.

Third+ Chance
A third or later follow-up scoring opportunity in the same attacking sequence, manually tagged when the attack produces another distinct attempt after earlier attempts.

Traffic
A shot manually tagged when one or more players meaningfully obstruct or complicate the goalie's view or ability to track the puck. Mere player presence near the net is not sufficient.

Low-Value Shot
A shot manually tagged as presenting limited scoring threat under Hockey Untapped's shot-quality criteria, considering factors such as distance, angle, pressure, and whether the attempt has a realistic path to the net.
