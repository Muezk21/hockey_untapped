HOCKEY UNTAPPED — METRIC DEFINITIONS (V4)

CORE DISTINCTION
- A zone entry and a possession are not interchangeable.
- Carry-in = manually recorded Anaheim controlled carry across the offensive blue line.
- Only Carry-in starts the entry-to-shot clock.
- DZ → OZ Transition Completed and Stretch Pass → OZ Possession are descriptive puck-movement events. They do NOT start the entry clock.
- Takeaways / opponent giveaways can start an Anaheim tracked possession sequence, but do NOT count as zone entries.
- Opponent takeaways / Anaheim giveaways end the Anaheim tracked sequence.
- Dump-ins and uncontrolled entries are not counted as carry-ins.
- Track Anaheim entries only for this study.

SHOT TIMING
- Timing is measured from the most recent recorded Anaheim Carry-in to the first shot in that tracked sequence.
- Rush: 0–4.99 seconds.
- Early: 5–10 seconds inclusive.
- Sustained: >10–30 seconds inclusive.
- Extended: >30 seconds.
- A shot before a recorded carry-in is Unclassified for entry timing. Never infer an entry.
- Follow-up shots remain Follow-up; their Second_Chance / Third_Plus_Chance tags are separate shot-quality modifiers and are not extra entries.
- Timing classes describe the first shot after a carry-in; do not interpret them as timing labels for every shot in a sequence.

IDENTIFIERS IN V4 EXPORT
- Possession_ID is a tracker-generated ID for a tracked Anaheim sequence. It is not a complete NHL possession reconstruction: faceoffs, all loose-puck recoveries, and every change of control are not fully modeled.
- Entry_ID uniquely identifies a recorded Carry-in and is the authoritative link for entry-based timing.
- Use Entry_ID plus nonnegative Seconds_Since_Entry to validate entry-timing rows.
- Use Possession_ID only for tracked-sequence summaries, and label those summaries as tracked sequences rather than all game possessions.
- Event_ID is the NHL play-by-play event identifier for API events.
- IDs are regenerated deterministically when the tracker reclassifies the current game; export the CSV after finishing manual event logging.

SCORING CHANCE AND GOAL OUTCOMES
- Scoring chance is a manually applied shot tag (hybrid workflow); a chance tag on a manual event is not a shot.
- Actual goals are counted from Shot_Result = Goal, not Led_To_Goal.
- Led_To_Goal is an attribution marker only and must never be used as a goal counter.
- Second_Chance and Third_Plus_Chance are shot-level modifiers. Report their denominators as tagged shots, not all events.
- Traffic and Low_Value are overlapping shot descriptors. Do not add their counts to timing-group totals.

DEFENSIVE / TURNOVER EVENTS
- OZ Turnover Forced is a manually recorded event; a subsequent chance/shot should be attributed only when it shares a valid tracked-sequence ID.
- D-Man Gap Close and Activated D-Man are manual observations. Temporal proximity alone is association, not proof of causation.
- Do not claim a D-Man action caused a turnover or scoring chance without a linked sequence and a defensible comparison group.

LEGACY DATA
- Older raw CSVs do not contain recorded carry-in events, Entry_ID, or trustworthy possession IDs.
- Historical Rush / Early / Sustained / Extended labels are legacy manually assigned categories, not measured time from an observed carry-in.
- Keep legacy observations separate from V4 entry-based metrics.
- Do not calculate legacy possession outcomes or entry conversion rates.
- Historical sample size is small and game-clustered; descriptive results only, no predictive claims.

RECOMMENDED REPORTING
- Shot-level: scoring-chance rate and actual-goal rate, with n and uncertainty interval.
- Entry-level: entries with at least one shot / all recorded carry-ins; entries with at least one chance / all recorded carry-ins; entries with a goal / all recorded carry-ins. Keep the denominator at entries.
- Tracked-sequence level: share with a shot, share with a chance, share with a goal, and shots per tracked sequence. Explicitly state the sequence coverage limitation above.
- Defensive-action level: report counts and linked outcomes only where the same tracked-sequence ID supports the link. Otherwise label temporal matches as descriptive associations.
- Treat n<20 as highly unstable; n<50 as exploratory. Require more games and repeated observations before predictive modeling.
